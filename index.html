1. Introduction

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is known as the Red Planet. It has a cold, dry, and rocky environment. Its surface contains mountains, valleys, volcanoes, impact craters, and large areas covered with dust.

More Information About Mars

Mars is smaller than Earth and has two natural moons called Phobos and Deimos. A day on Mars lasts approximately 24 hours and 39 minutes. A year on Mars is about 687 Earth days.

Scientists believe that liquid water existed on the surface of Mars billions of years ago. Evidence of ancient rivers and lakes can still be found in its geological formations.

Interesting Facts

  • Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun.
  • It is called the Red Planet.
  • It has two small moons.
  • Its atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide.
