5. Environmental Conditions

Mars has a very thin atmosphere, mainly composed of carbon dioxide. Its surface experiences extreme temperatures, strong winds, and dust storms.

More Information

The Martian atmosphere is much thinner than Earth's atmosphere. It cannot provide enough protection from harmful radiation or maintain warm temperatures.

Temperature

Mars is a very cold planet. Its average temperature is approximately -63 degrees Celsius. Temperatures can change depending on the location and time of day.

Atmosphere

The atmosphere of Mars is made mostly of carbon dioxide. It also contains small amounts of nitrogen and argon.

Dust Storms

Strong winds can lift dust from the surface and create large dust storms. Sometimes these storms cover enormous areas of the planet.

Environmental Challenges

  • Very low temperatures.
  • Thin atmosphere.
  • Strong winds.
  • Dust storms.
  • Radiation exposure.
