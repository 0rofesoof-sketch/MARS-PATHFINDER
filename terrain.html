4. Terrain and Elevation

The Martian surface has different elevations and slopes. Some areas are relatively flat, while others contain steep slopes, rocky surfaces, and deep depressions.

More Information

Mars has mountains, valleys, plains, highlands, and large basins. Its northern hemisphere is generally lower than its southern highlands.

Elevation refers to the height of a location compared to a reference level. Scientists use elevation measurements to create topographic maps of Mars.

Importance of Terrain Analysis

  • Helps identify safe landing locations.
  • Helps robotic rovers avoid steep slopes.
  • Shows the height of mountains and valleys.
  • Helps scientists study the planet's geological history.

Topographic Maps

Topographic maps use different colours to represent different elevations. They help researchers understand the shape of the Martian surface.
