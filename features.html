3. Major Geological Features

Mars contains several important geological features:

Olympus Mons

Olympus Mons is the largest known volcano in the Solar System. It is a giant shield volcano with very wide and gentle slopes. It is much taller than Mount Everest.

Valles Marineris

Valles Marineris is a huge canyon system on Mars. It stretches for thousands of kilometres and is much larger than the Grand Canyon on Earth.

Impact Craters

Impact craters are circular depressions formed when asteroids or meteorites collide with the Martian surface. They help scientists understand the history of Mars.

Sand Dunes

Sand dunes are formed when wind moves sand particles across the surface. Their shapes and locations show how Martian winds change the landscape.

Why Are These Features Important?

Studying geological features helps scientists understand the planet's formation, volcanic activity, impacts, and climate history.
