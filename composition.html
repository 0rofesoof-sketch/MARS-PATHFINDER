2. Surface Composition

The surface of Mars consists of rocks, minerals, dust, and loose soil called regolith. Its reddish appearance is mainly caused by iron minerals that have oxidized (rusted).

More Information

Mars contains different types of rocks, including volcanic rocks. These rocks provide information about the planet's geological history. The surface also contains fine dust that can be carried by strong winds.

Iron oxide gives Mars its famous reddish color. Scientists use special instruments on spacecraft and robotic rovers to study the minerals and chemical elements found in Martian soil.

Main Surface Materials

  • Rocks
  • Iron-rich minerals
  • Dust
  • Regolith
  • Volcanic materials
